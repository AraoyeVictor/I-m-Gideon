MY STORY

Hey, I'm Gideon Adeojo.

I'm a web designer and creative problem-solver who believes great design should feel natural. I turn ideas into thoughtful, welcoming websites through strong visual direction, considered layouts, and attention to the details people notice.

Over the past four years, I've partnered with small teams and growing businesses to shape early ideas into polished visual experiences. I love a good design challenge, a well-balanced layout, and the moment when every detail feels just right.

When I'm away from my desk, you'll usually find me exploring a new neighborhood, collecting ideas in my notebook, or trying a recipe that may or may not work.
